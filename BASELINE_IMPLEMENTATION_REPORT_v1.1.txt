ARITHMETIC BOUND
B = 16*epsilon*S + 8*eta, with eta the smallest positive binary64 subnormal and
S = max(max(abs(u)), abs(c_mid), abs(c_final), abs(delta)). No fitted tolerance.
For finite nonoverflowing values, one rounded add/subtract at magnitude <=2S
has error <=epsilon*S+eta/2. Halving is exact except possible subnormal rounding.
The implemented comparison chains (rounded midpoint, final addition, point
subtractions, shift/range/imbalance differences) conservatively require less
than 11*epsilon*S+5*eta by magnitude bounds through those operations. The fixed
16/8 bound therefore covers them. Zero is checked exactly; no range division.
Maximum observed real-fixture error/B: 0.10577906479272349
This bound never replaces exact Decimal comparison of exported D and E.
