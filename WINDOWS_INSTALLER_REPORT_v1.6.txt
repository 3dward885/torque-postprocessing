Torque Data Postprocessing 1.6 — implementation and validation
6 October 2026; installer build 4

Scope: fixed-field review with explicit entire-scan decisions. No parser,
segmentation, coordinate rounding, subtraction, centering or molar formula change.
Single agent; no commits, pushes, GitHub writes or running-app control.

USER-AUTHORIZED RULE
Full original acquired measured span max(B)-min(B), Oe/10000 converted using
Decimal. Round span to 0.01 T (nearest; ties away from zero); review at or above
the configured per-file limit. User supplied 0.5 T and explicitly approved this
rounded >= rule. It supersedes the original exact > comparison. Effective
unrounded boundary at that limit is 0.495 T. Raw/export field values unchanged.
No experimental limit is built into defaults; blank is unconfigured.

IMPLEMENTATION
field_quality.py supplies versioned policy, full-row evidence and bound decisions.
pipeline.py and cli.py share enforcement; partial processing never enables final
export while an included acquisition is unresolved. Backgrounds are checked too.
app.py shows a persistent warning and per-scan state. Review scan opens original
rows and a measured field trace. Closing/cancelling has no exclusion effect.
Explicit exclusions remove the whole acquisition from treated outputs but retain
every raw row and reason. Changed source, scan or policy requires new review.
An open panel cannot accept a decision after its displayed evidence has changed.
Help covers limits, warnings, migration, source evidence and physical limitations.
